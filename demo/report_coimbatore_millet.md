# Competitor Report: Healthy millet meal boxes delivered for lunch, Coimbatore

**Target customer:** Office workers, age 25 to 40

The best starting offer is a healthy millet lunch box for office workers in Coimbatore, with a simple weekday delivery promise and clean, filling food. Competitor reviews show the main openings are not fancy recipes but reliable lunch service: better portion size, fresher food, on-time delivery, correct items, and cleaner packing.

## 3. Market Gap

The clearest gap is a dependable office lunch plan for healthy millet meal boxes that is priced below the most expensive healthy office option but still feels more value-focused than a one-off order. Competitors either publish a high per-meal office plan, sell generic home meals, or attract complaints about high price, low quantity, stale food, wrong orders, slow service, or cleanliness.

*Evidence: The Healthy Craft, Zero Cals, Kovai Kitchen, Health Box Kitchen.*

## 4. Recommended Positioning

> **Healthy millet lunch boxes for office workers — ₹199 per meal**

**Launch price:** Rs 199 per meal. This sits below The Healthy Craft’s ₹229 per meal office plan and above Health Box Kitchen’s ₹100 per order, while avoiding the premium-price complaint tied to The Healthy Craft. It also gives a clearer office-lunch positioning than the monthly home-meal pricing at ₹4000 and ₹5038.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan

**Week 1: Check demand and lock the first offer**
- Create 2 millet lunch box menus for office lunch, with one veg option and one more filling option.
- Visit 3 office areas in Coimbatore and speak to workers about their lunch habits and what they dislike about current delivery food.
- Show the recommended lunch box idea and ask which menu they would try first.
- Collect at least 20 names and phone numbers from people who want a trial lunch.

**Week 2: Run the first small delivery trial**
- Deliver 10 to 15 trial lunch boxes to the contacts from week 1.
- Keep one fixed lunch delivery time and note every late, missing, or wrong order.
- Ask each person for short feedback on taste, quantity, freshness, and packing.
- Write down which menu gets the most repeat interest.

**Week 3: Fix the weak points from trial feedback**
- Improve the box using the top 2 complaints from the trial, such as portion size, temperature, or packing.
- Make the packing cleaner and easier to carry to office desks.
- Offer a simple weekday lunch plan to the people who said they would order again.
- Visit 2 more office locations and leave flyers or WhatsApp details with guards or reception desks.

**Week 4: Test repeat orders and decide the next move**
- Deliver a second round to the people who already tried the box.
- Ask 10 trial customers to place a 5-day lunch order if they liked the first meal.
- Collect feedback on why people reorder or why they say no.
- Decide whether to keep the same menu, improve the menu again, or focus only on the office areas with the strongest response.
