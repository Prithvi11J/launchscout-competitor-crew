# Competitor Report: Evening and weekend dance classes (Bollywood, hip-hop, dance fitness), Chennai

**Target customer:** Working professionals, age 22 to 40

The best opening is a simple adult dance membership in Chennai for working professionals, with evening and weekend batches, beginner-friendly teaching, and the full price shown upfront. This stands out because nearby competitors often hide pricing, push longer commitments, or create confusion with unclear charges and slow replies, so your edge is trust and convenience.

## 3. Market Gap

The clearest gap is a transparent, easy-to-join evening-and-weekend adult program for working professionals that shows the full price up front and avoids hidden-fee anxiety. Existing Chennai options either publish only enquiry-based pricing, bundle multi-month commitments, or attract complaints about unanswered calls and unclear charges, so none stands out as a simple, low-friction choice for someone who wants to start after work or on weekends.

*Evidence: Dance and Dance Studio, The Swingers Dance, Unitedbydance Community, Raack Dance Academy.*

## 4. Recommended Positioning

> **After-work Bollywood, hip-hop & dance fitness for working professionals — ₹4,000 per month, evenings + weekends, beginner-friendly, no hidden fees.**

**Launch price:** Rs 4,000 per month. ₹4,000 per month matches the only clearly stated monthly competitor price in the research and stays within the believable market range flagged by the competitor data. It is positioned as a simple monthly membership against competitors that use per-course pricing, enquiry-only pricing, or have complaints about unclear charges.

## 5. 30-Day Launch Plan

**Week 1: Set up the offer and test the message.**
- Write one clear offer sheet: after-work Bollywood, hip-hop, and dance fitness for working professionals, evenings and weekends, beginner-friendly, with no hidden fees.
- Create one WhatsApp message and one simple poster with the same offer and timing details.
- Ask 15 people in your target group to react to the offer and say what feels unclear or unappealing.
- Make one short Google Form or landing page to collect interest.

**Week 2: Reach working adults where they already are.**
- Visit 3 office areas, tech parks, or nearby cafes after work hours and share flyers or QR codes.
- Post the offer in 10 local WhatsApp, Facebook, or apartment groups.
- Message 20 warm contacts and ask each to share the offer with one friend who works full time.
- Collect at least 30 enquiries or sign-ups from the outreach.

**Week 3: Run trial classes and test interest.**
- Run 2 trial classes, one on a weekday evening and one on a weekend.
- Keep each class 45 to 60 minutes, beginner-friendly, and focused on fun, fitness, and confidence.
- At the end of each class, ask attendees to join the monthly plan or confirm within 24 hours.
- Ask every attendee to fill a short feedback form about timing, style, and price clarity.

**Week 4: Refine the offer and convert first members.**
- Review what people liked most and remove any confusing points from the offer and flyer.
- Follow up with everyone who attended or enquired and ask for one clear answer: join now or not now.
- Announce a clear start date for the next batch and keep the first batch small and easy to join.
- Aim to convert the first 10 to 20 paid members and note the top 3 reasons they joined.
